09

Screening Methodology

How this prototype evaluates the submitted image.

Image preprocessing

The uploaded retinal image is converted into a standardized RGB representation and resized before analysis.

Retinal region analysis

Background regions with very low brightness are excluded from the primary pixel-level analysis.

Pattern detection

Pixel-level rules are used to identify hemorrhage-like, exudate-like, and vessel contrast patterns.

Risk scoring

The detected pattern ratios are combined into a screening score which determines whether the image requires review.

Important Limitations

Information that should be considered when interpreting this report.

  • RetinaScan is a screening and decision-support prototype, not a replacement for professional medical diagnosis.
  • Image quality, lighting, focus, camera characteristics, and retinal field of view can influence automated image analysis.
  • A normal screening result does not guarantee the absence of retinal disease.
  • A flagged result does not by itself establish a clinical diagnosis. Professional examination is required for confirmation.
